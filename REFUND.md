# Refund Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

## First payment

You can get a full refund within 14 days of the first payment of a subscription. Email support@dops.media to request it. Once the refund is issued, the license key is disabled.

## Renewals

You can cancel at any time; your access continues to the end of the paid period. Renewal payments are refundable within 7 days if you did not use the app after the renewal, or if there is a technical problem we cannot fix.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds are handled by our reseller / Merchant of Record and returned to the original payment method.

## Chargebacks

Please contact support before raising a chargeback — it is usually faster for you. A chargeback raised without contacting us may lead to the license key being deactivated.
