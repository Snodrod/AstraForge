# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

AstraForge is operated by Artem Doronin, an individual trading as "Dops Media", based in Bali, Indonesia. This policy explains what data we process, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

## What we process and why

**Buyer email** — to send your license key and to contact you about your subscription.

**License key data** — a hash of the key and its last 4 characters, plus subscription status and dates. Used to activate and validate your license and to manage renewals.

**Hashed computer identifier and optional computer name** — a SHA-256 hash of the Windows MachineGuid, used to bind one license key to one computer and to allow transfers.

**IP address in server logs** — used for abuse protection and rate limiting.

We do not sell your data. There is no advertising and no tracking in the app. Generation runs locally on your computer, and no user content is uploaded to our servers for generation.

## Who processes it

- **Our reseller / Merchant of Record** (for example Polar or Creem, and lava.top for Russia) handles payments, taxes and invoices. We never see full card numbers.
- **Resend** sends the emails that contain your license key.
- **Cloudflare** sits in front of our key server, which is hosted on our own machine.

These providers process data on our behalf for the purposes above.

## Retention

Data is kept while your subscription is active, plus up to 3 years for accounting purposes. After that it is deleted, or deleted earlier on request.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **correct** data that is wrong;
- **delete** your data.

To make a request, email support@dops.media. We may need to confirm your identity — for example, by writing from the email address associated with your license. Some data may need to be kept for accounting or legal reasons; we will tell you if that applies.
